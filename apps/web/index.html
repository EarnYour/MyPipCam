Local-first screen and camera picture-in-picture recording — a Chrome extension for capture and editing, plus a macOS companion for an always-on-top camera bubble.

View on GitHub Privacy Terms

What it does

Record your screen or tab with a live camera PiP, keep clips in a local library, and optionally connect Google Drive or OpenAI transcription — only when you choose to.

  • Chrome extension

    Tab or screen capture with a draggable camera PiP, local library, trim/cut editor, and optional Drive upload.

  • macOS companion

    Floating always-on-top camera window for OBS and desktop recording workflows.

  • Open source

    Free MIT-licensed software. Source, builds, and docs live on GitHub.
